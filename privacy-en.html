Block Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you play Block Rush ("the game"), developed by Ahmet Görkem Duran ("the developer", "we").

1. Information we collect

The game does not directly collect personal data. You don't need an account, and you are never asked for your name, email or phone number. Only the advertising service described below (Google AdMob) collects some technical information from your device itself.

2. Data stored on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the following is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us:

  • Game progress, scores and records
  • Coins, emeralds, boosters, themes and costumes
  • Sound, vibration and language settings

    • Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes this information permanently. If your device backup is turned on, Android may back this data up to your Google account; that is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

    3. Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback while you play. It is a standard Android permission granted at install; you can turn vibration off in the game settings at any time.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used to load ads and to connect to the store.

    • 4. Ads

    The game uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example to continue a game). AdMob may collect the advertising ID, device and app information, the IP address and an approximate location based on it, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions such as the EEA and the UK your consent for personalized ads is asked when the game starts; you can change it any time in Settings > Ad preferences. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

    5. Purchases

    In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. If you bought Premium, it is restored automatically through your Google Play account when you reinstall the game.

    6. Children's privacy

    The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

    7. Your rights

    Your rights under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and similar laws are reserved. As the developer we keep no data about you on any server; your game data stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game. For requests about advertising data you can use Google's privacy tools or write to us.

    8. Changes

    This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available in the game's settings menu, with the date of the last update at the top.
